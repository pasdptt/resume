# Short Biography

Pasd Putthapipat, PhD, is a data and AI leader who built the analytics and products department at SCB DataX, led 30+ data scientists at True Digital Group, and most recently led Data and AI Platform development at Infinitas by Krungthai. His experience combines product strategy, organization building, data governance expertise, and university teaching and departmental leadership. He holds a PhD in Electrical Engineering from Florida International University and has been on a career break since August 2026.
