# Short Biography

Pasd Putthapipat, PhD, is a data and AI executive and university lecturer. His experience includes Acting Director of Data responsibility for a virtual banking initiative at Infinitas by Krungthai, founding-team involvement and department building at DataX, and leadership of 30+ data scientists at True Digital Group. He combines data platform, governance, and AI product experience with adjunct teaching and published virtual-banking research. He has been on a career break from corporate roles since August 2026.
