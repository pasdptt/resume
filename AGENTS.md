6. Achievement Format

Whenever possible, write achievements using:

Action + Scale + Business Impact

Example:

Established an enterprise Data Platform Office covering data engineering, architecture, governance, analytics, and regulatory reporting, enabling scalable enterprise data services and AI adoption.

Better when metrics are available:

Led a 20-person Data & AI organization and established a cloud-native enterprise data platform supporting regulatory reporting, analytics, and AI workloads across multiple business domains.

Never fabricate metrics.

If the source does not contain a reliable number, write the achievement without inventing one.

Mark uncertain information internally for review rather than guessing.
